FAQ

Questions we hear often

How long does a website take?

Usually 1 to 3 weeks once we have your content and photos. Stores and apps take longer. We give you a clear timeline at the quote stage.

Do you handle domain and hosting?

Yes. We set up the domain, hosting and renewals. The domain stays registered in your name.

Will it work well on phones?

Yes. Every site is designed for phones first, then checked on tablets and computers.

Can I update the content myself?

Yes. We can add a simple admin panel. If you prefer, send us the changes and we update it for you.

Do you support us after launch?

Yes. Every package includes a support period, and after that you can choose a monthly or yearly maintenance plan.
